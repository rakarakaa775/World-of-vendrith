# WORLD Ground Transitions / Edge & Blend Library Audit — 2026-09-26\n\n## Scope\nThis audit maps transition and blend candidates between natural WORLD materials. A transition asset is a relationship between materials, not a new biome.\n\n## Repository enumeration\nA focused filename scan found 29 transition-related PNG candidates.\n\n### Strong transition families\n- Grass/other: `grassalt`, `grassgrassaltother`, plus hole/depression grass variants.\n- Grass ↔ Water: `watergrass`, `watergrassaltother`.\n- Sand ↔ Water / Red Sand: `sandwater`, `sandredsandwater`, `redsandwater`, `waterredsandother`, `watersandother`.\n- Snow ↔ Grass / Water / Ice: `Snow Grass Transitions`, `watersnowgrass`, `snowwater`, `snowice`, `icesnowgrass`.\n- Cold Water ↔ Ground / Snow: `coldwatergrass`, `coldwatergrassaltother`, `coldwatersandother`, `coldwaterredsandother`, `coldwatersnowgrass`, `coldwatersnowother`.\n- Ice ↔ Grass: `icegrass`, `icegrassaltother`, `icesnowgrass`.\n- Lava ↔ Grass: `lavagrassaltother` is specialized volcanic transition, not generic Ground.\n\n## Canonical transition graph\nGRASS ↔ DIRT / STONE / SAND / BEACH / WATER\nSAND ↔ WATER / RED SAND\nSNOW ↔ GRASS / WATER / ICE\nCOLD WATER ↔ GRASS / SAND / SNOW / ICE\nICE ↔ GRASS / SNOW\nWET GROUND ↔ WATER / BRACKISH WATER\nLAVA ↔ GRASS\n\nNot every theoretical edge has a dedicated binary. The graph represents logical compatibility.\n\n## Source findings\n[LPC] Terrains was explicitly designed for seamless terrain composition.
